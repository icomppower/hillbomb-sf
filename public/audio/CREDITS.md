# Audio credits

Every sound in this folder is a real recording from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), released under
**Creative Commons 0** (public domain dedication: no attribution required; credited here anyway). Nothing is
synthesised. Source: each sound's Freesound high-quality preview.

Processing (`tools/audio/build-hillbomb.mjs`): excerpt chosen from the signal (loops: the steadiest window of
momentary loudness; one-shots: the loudest event, or a window picked from the loudness envelope), fades, an
equal-power crossfade at the loop point, loudness normalisation (loops −23 LUFS integrated, one-shots peak −1 dBFS),
Opus in Ogg. The table below gives each excerpt's start and length in the source. `wind`, `pier_lap` and `gull` are
kept from SF Bay Crossing (same licence).
